Delete Your Account in the App

  1. Log in to the LUWAS mobile application.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Read and confirm the deletion warning.
  5. Enter your current password.
  6. Select the delete button to permanently delete the account.

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. The in-app process signs you out and clears locally stored account/session data after the server confirms deletion.

What Is Deleted or Removed

  • Username and account identity
  • Name, email address, and phone number
  • Household profile information associated with your account
  • Personal settings and notification preferences
  • Personal emergency-request information where deletion is legally and operationally allowed
  • Local app session and cached account data

Records That May Be Retained

Some records may need to be retained or anonymized for emergency-response documentation, evacuation and public-safety records, legal or regulatory requirements, security, fraud prevention, or dispute resolution. When retained, your direct personal identity will be removed or minimized where possible.
